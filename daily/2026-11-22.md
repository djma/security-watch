## Servers & dev tools

- **GPGME CVE-2026-129975 (critical): a crafted OpenPGP key with a very long user ID triggers a double free.** GnuPG says it can be used to exfiltrate data from apps (for example by getting them to send signed data), and RCE "can't be ruled out". It affects every GPGME before 2.3.1, which sits under most mail clients, many XMPP clients, Gpg4win, GnuPG Desktop, Samba and Flatpak. Disclosure was moved up to Nov 20. No exploitation reported. **Do:** update the gpgme library to **2.3.1** (or your distro's patched package), update **Gpg4win** on Windows, and rebuild anything statically linked. Until then, don't import keys from strangers. [GnuPG advisory](https://lists.gnupg.org/pipermail/gnupg-announce/2026q4/000517.html) · [T8557](https://dev.gnupg.org/T8557) · [X post](https://x.com/DailyDarkWeb/status/2124219548861702339)

- **Upload2Shell (Ghostscript CVE-2026-125396): full write-up published Nov 21.** A use-after-free in Ghostscript's Type 1 font loader escapes the `-dSAFER` sandbox. Because ImageMagick hands PDF/PS/EPS content to Ghostscript even when a file is named `.png` or `.jpg`, the researchers got code execution through WordPress uploads, Roundcube attachment thumbnails (and Plesk), shared CUPS print queues on Debian 13 (unauthenticated, as `lp`), DokuWiki, Mayan EDMS and Google's document renderer. The fix has been out since **Ghostscript 10.08.0** (Sep 8). No in-the-wild exploitation reported, but the details are now public. **Do:**
  - Run `gs --version` on servers and desktops and make sure it is **10.08.0+** or your distro's patched build.
  - On anything that converts or thumbnails untrusted uploads, block the `PS`, `EPS`, `PDF` and `XPS` coders in ImageMagick's `policy.xml` if you don't need them.
  - Don't share CUPS printers on untrusted networks.

  [pwn.ai write-up](https://pwn.ai/upload2shell) · [X: @PaulosYibelo](https://x.com/PaulosYibelo/status/2124203995329933441)

- **OpenRouter: prompts sent to MiniMax models were read by unknown third parties.** A researcher put unique links in prompts routed through OpenRouter to MiniMax, and someone opened them 40–130 hours later. Some providers (MiniMax, Novita, StreamLake, AtlasCloud) forwarded traffic to MiniMax even on endpoints labeled "Zero Data Retention". OpenRouter has removed that label from those endpoints and is emailing affected customers; one notice covers requests from **Sep 23 to Oct 19 (UTC)**. This follows yesterday's Dragos study of AI providers leaking secrets. **Do:** check for an OpenRouter email; rotate any key, password or token that appeared in prompts or tool outputs sent to MiniMax or ERNIE models; restrict routing to providers you trust. [X: notice screenshot](https://x.com/MiaAI_lab/status/2124054476893851731) · [X: summary](https://x.com/michael_chomsky/status/2124008002571428224)

## Crypto & scams

- **Chainflip's Ethereum vault was drained of $4.86M (Nov 21)**: 520 ETH, 3.25M USDC and 758K USDT in one transaction, which reports attribute to a compromised validator signing key rather than a contract bug. The funds went into Tornado Cash. Chainflip is in full safe mode, so deposits, withdrawals and swaps are off. **Do:** don't route swaps through Chainflip (directly or via wallets that use it) until it reopens, and ignore "refund/claim" links that aren't from @Chainflip. [Chainflip on X](https://x.com/Chainflip/status/2124068986845532605) · [Lookonchain](https://x.com/lookonchain/status/2124125612617138670)
- **Cyber (CYBER) bridge exploit:** 5.21M CYBER was unexpectedly minted on Solana and the same amount left the Ethereum LayerZero bridge lockbox; about $968K was sold for ETH. Upbit and Bithumb froze CYBER deposits and withdrawals. The cause isn't known yet. **Do:** don't use the CYBER bridge or send CYBER to exchanges until Cyber gives the all-clear. [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-11-21-cyber-investigating-suspected-1-million-bridge-exploit-as-upbit-bithumb-freeze-transfers-444810)
- **Bitcoin Core CVE-2026-117348 (medium):** a malicious peer can stall a node for more than 12 seconds per message using duplicate orphan announcements. No theft risk, and not exploited. **Do:** if you run a node, upgrade to **30.5 / 31.3 / 32.1**. 29.x gets no fix. [Bitcoin Core disclosure](https://bitcoincore.org/en/2026/11/20/disclose-orphan-inv-stall/)
- **Still live:** pam-mysql SSH bypass and the PHP phpAfterShell PoCs ([2026-11-21](https://djma.github.io/security-watch/daily/2026-11-21.md)), NGINX2Root ([2026-11-19](https://djma.github.io/security-watch/daily/2026-11-19.md)), the unfixed ClickHouse RowBinary bug ([2026-11-20](https://djma.github.io/security-watch/daily/2026-11-20.md)), and tampered Ledgers sold by third-party retailers.
